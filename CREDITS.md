# Credits

Dolphin for PS4 stands on the work of many people and projects. Thank you.

## Dolphin for PS4

**ShiroKlein**: the PS4 port. The PS4 platform layer, the Vulkan path on the PS4 GPU, the XMB
menu and in-game menu, the settings, the start-up animation and the testing on real hardware.

## Dolphin

Every game runs on **[Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org)**, the GameCube and Wii emulator by the
Dolphin Emulator Project (GPL-2.0-or-later). This port builds Dolphin from revision
`771fb154059c5812d6a715a23226249cc42877a2` with one patch,
[`patches/dolphin-ps4.patch`](patches/dolphin-ps4.patch).

Dolphin was started in 2003 by **F|RES** and **ector**, who released its source in 2008. Since
then hundreds of people have built it into what it is today. These are the Dolphin contributors
listed on GitHub, most commits first; the
[full list](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/graphs/contributors) and Dolphin's history
include many more.

## Graphics on the PS4

| Project | Role | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| [Mesa](https://mesa3d.org) and its **RADV** Vulkan driver | Every frame goes through RADV, built for the PS4 with a GNM winsys ([`port/mesa`](port/mesa), [`patches/mesa-ps4.patch`](patches/mesa-ps4.patch), based on Mesa 26.0.8) | MIT and per-file licences |
| [Dear ImGui](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui) by Omar Cornut | The XMB menu and the in-game menu are drawn with it (Dolphin ships it) | MIT |
